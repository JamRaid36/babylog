      Welcome to Jubilee

      Keeping track is two taps. Here's all there is to it.

      • Tap Feed, Pee or Poop the moment it happens, then tap Save. Nothing is logged until you tap Save.
      • On a Feed, tap Left or Right for the side it started on, before Save. Jubilee then tells you which side is next.
      • Forgot one? Tap it now, tap 15 min, 30 min or 1 hour, then Save.
      • Made a mistake? Tap Undo, or tap any entry to fix or delete it.

      How this works

      Logging

      1. Tap Feed, Pee or Poop at the bottom the moment it happens.
      2. Tap Save. It keeps the time of your first tap. Nothing is logged until you tap Save, so a stray tap does no harm: tap Discard.
      3. Walked away without saving? It is not lost. It waits at the top of the front page, asking to be saved or discarded.

      After a Feed, two big buttons ask which side she started on: tap Left or Right (or Bottle). One tap, no typing. The front page then tells you which side to start on next time.

      After a Bottle feed or a Pump, the tray asks how much. Tap oz or mL first, whichever you measure in; this phone remembers. Then tap an amount, or nudge it to the exact number. For a bottle of donor milk, tap Donor milk too.

      The front page adds up what she drank from bottles in the last 24 hours, and each day in the list has its own total. A bottle with no amount is called out, so you know the total is short.

      The little tray that pops up also lets you Undo or say it happened earlier. Ignore it and it goes away.

      Forgot to log one?

      1. Tap the button now.
      2. In the tray, tap 15 min, 30 min or 1 hour.
      3. For any other time, tap Add a note or exact time.

      Fix or delete an entry

      1. Tap the entry in History.
      2. Change what you need and tap Save entry.
      3. To remove it, tap Delete this entry, then tap it again.

      What the top of the screen shows

      Last feed, Last pee, Last poop is how long ago each one was. Last 24 hours is how many of each since this time yesterday.

      Sharing with the other phone

      The words under the baby's name tell you where your entries are:

      • This phone only: entries stay on this phone. Tap those words to connect it.
      • Shared: both phones see the same log.
      • Syncing: sending right now.
      • Not synced: usually no signal. Your entries are safe and it keeps trying. Tap it to try again.

      To invite another phone: tap the gear, then Invite another phone, and text the link. That's all you do.

      Were you sent an invite? Tap the link in the text. Jubilee walks you through the rest, one step at a time.
